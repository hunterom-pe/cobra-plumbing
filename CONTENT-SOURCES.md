## Logo

`assets/reference/logo-comparison-board.webp` is the supplied comparison board
(1536 × 1024), kept unmodified. The chosen direction is B1 / Original Character
(left column).

`scripts/extract-logo.py` crops the B1 column and makes the board's flat ivory
backdrop transparent. Nothing was redrawn, traced, or upscaled.

| File | Pixels | Largest display size on the page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/img/logo-emblem.*` | 400 × 438 | 216 px tall (hero plate) |
| `assets/img/logo-stacked.*` | 448 × 594 | 224 px wide (About) |
| `assets/img/favicon.png` | 180 × 180 | Browser tab |

The display sizes are capped at half the crop size so the artwork is never
enlarged on 2x screens. On 3x phone screens the About logo is shown at about
192 px wide, which needs 576 px and so is slightly soft.

These are **interim raster assets**. No vector file of the B1 logo was
supplied. A faithful vector master, drawn or approved by the logo's designer,
is needed before production use, print, or any larger placement. The crops
have a faint ivory edge, so they are only placed on ivory backgrounds.
