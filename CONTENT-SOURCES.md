## Left out pending owner confirmation

- **Years in business.** The About page says 17 years in its header and 19 in its body.
- **Review count.** "Over 60 5-star reviews on Google" is self-reported and unverified.
- **Discounts.** 10% off for veterans and seniors (Home).
- **Warranties and guarantee.** "Warranties on our services and products" and "we guarantee all our work" (Home, About).
- **Emergency service.** The source mentions weekend and night emergency availability and a "rapid response team". The concept makes no emergency, after-hours, or response-time claim.
- **Pricing policy.** "Charging by the job, not by the hour" (Home, About).
- **Street address.** 1431 E Osborn Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85014 is published, but the concept lists only "Phoenix, Arizona" as a service location until the owner confirms whether the address should be shown and how.
- **People and media.** The About page names the CEO and mentions radio segments and a YouTube channel. Not used.
- **License, bond, and insurance details.** None are published on the source pages. None are shown.
- **Service area beyond Phoenix.** No other cities are published. None are shown.

## Logo

`assets/reference/logo-comparison-board.webp` is the supplied comparison board,
kept unmodified. The chosen direction is B1 / Original Character (left column).

`assets/img/logo-stacked.*`, `logo-emblem.*`, and `favicon.png` are straight
crops of the B1 column produced by `scripts/extract-logo.py`, with the board's
flat ivory backdrop made transparent. Nothing was redrawn or traced.

These are **interim raster assets** (about 450 px wide). A production vector
master of the B1 logo is still needed before any real use. The crops have a
faint ivory edge, so they are only placed on ivory backgrounds.
