About

A Phoenix plumbing company focused on repairs.

Cobra Plumbing LLC is based in Phoenix, Arizona. Residential plumbing is the main focus: the repairs, replacements, and drain problems that come up in a home.

Drain cleaning is the company’s specialty, from a single clogged drain to hydrojetting and video inspection of a sewer line. Cobra also takes on light commercial plumbing for Phoenix businesses.
